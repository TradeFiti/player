Player profile

Joseph Omondi Ojwang is a Kenyan attacking midfielder who wears #10 and studies in Year 10 at Kitengela International School.

This profile gathers his footage, photographs from matches and training, documentation of his trial participation with Cambrils UCF in Catalonia, Spain, and the figures from one recorded technical training session. Enquiries go to his representative, Martin Lugendo.
